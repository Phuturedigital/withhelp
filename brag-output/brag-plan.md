# Brag Plan: WITH.

## What is this app?
WITH. is a South African personal safety network concept designed to start protection before an emergency by connecting protected journeys, trusted people, nearby help and response pathways.

## The angle
Show that WITH. is not another panic button. The video moves from the brand promise into the actual website language, then explains the product in three sharp ideas: protection starts earlier, the system connects multiple layers, and one failure should not end the safety chain. Keep the concept honest: this is a research prototype, not live nationwide coverage.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
“Safety should start before the emergency.”

A single coral signal appears on warm cream, followed by the headline with generous space.

## Key moments
- Recreate the real website hero and navigation inside a browser frame.
- Reveal the three product pillars: Guardian, Private Risk and Assistance Points.
- Show the no-single-point-of-failure network: Trusted Circle, nearby help and professional response remain connected even when one path drops.

## Outro / punchline
WITH.
Safety should start before the emergency.
withhelp.co.za

## User flow worth showing
None — the public website is primarily a concept and partnership storytelling surface. Use the strongest product concepts and actual website copy.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium public-safety concept film
- Interpretation: restrained pacing, clean typography, slow confident reveals, no fear-driven imagery and no exaggerated operational claims.

- Role: sparse professional support
- Music: locally generated ambient bed because the bundled Brag music assets are unavailable in this runtime
- Music treatment: low volume, soft fade in, slightly stronger lift at the product reveal, clean fade under the final logo
- Music cue guidance: unavailable

- SFX posture: sparse, soft UI-style chimes at scene transitions
- Restraint rule: no sirens, alarm sounds, cinematic danger hits or anxiety-inducing audio
